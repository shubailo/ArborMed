## 2024-05-19 - Standardized Tooltips in Flutter
**Learning:** In Flutter, `MaterialLocalizations.of(context)` provides built-in, localized strings like `previousPageTooltip` and `nextPageTooltip` that are perfect for standard pagination controls without needing custom translation entries.
**Action:** Always prefer using built-in `MaterialLocalizations` for common UI interactions (like pagination, back buttons, close buttons) to ensure consistent, natively translated accessibility labels.
