## 2024-04-01 - Dynamic Password Visibility Tooltips
**Learning:** In Flutter authentication forms, Icon-only buttons used for toggling password visibility must have explicit tooltips (e.g., 'Show password' / 'Hide password') that dynamically change based on the visibility state to meet accessibility requirements.
**Action:** Always ensure interactive icon-only elements provide screen reader context that accurately reflects their current state, not just their generic function.
